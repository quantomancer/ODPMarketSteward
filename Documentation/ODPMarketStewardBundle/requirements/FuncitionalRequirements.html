2. Product vision

ODP Market Steward shall be a professional ChatGPT plugin for governed market data. Its primary value is immediate access to the latest completed one-minute OHLC bars for the standard FX-35 set. Governed bundle discovery, deterministic validation, descriptive calculations, fitness evidence, and GPT-5.6 explanation shall augment the live data rather than obstruct access to it.

Input

The versioned ODPS-centred governed bundle, the public FXLive JSON routes, an authoritative UTC market-calendar state, an optional immutable recorded-evidence bundle, and a user's bounded professional question.

Output

A live market board or instrument view, deterministic OHLC metrics and quality results, traceable governed-bundle evidence, and a governed natural-language explanation.

Benefit

Users receive usable market data immediately and can understand whether the observed snapshot is complete, valid, timely, and suitable for a stated purpose.

Primary users

Analysts, governance and data-quality teams, and developers integrating or interrogating a governed market-data product.

Primary product promise “Show me the current FXLive market” shall produce market data first. Governance evidence shall be visible with the result and expandable through conversation.
Meaning of “MARKET DATA DEMO” The label defines the permitted data-use and financial-safety boundary. It shall not be used to excuse an incomplete, unreliable, or non-runnable application.

3. Scope and boundaries

In scope for the first working release

  • Latest completed one-minute OHLC bars for the exact FX-35 set.
  • Single-instrument detail and deterministic descriptive metrics.
  • Coverage, completeness, validity, consistency, and session-aware freshness evidence.
  • Governed product discovery with evidence references to the responsibility-owning bundle artifact.
  • GPT-5.6 governed explanations grounded in deterministic results.
  • Live, market-closed, degraded, unavailable, unknown, and recorded-evidence presentation.
  • A ChatGPT plugin containing an MCP-backed app/connector with five read-only domain tools, one component-only policy acknowledgement action, and a selective Apps SDK interface.

Explicitly out of scope

  • Streaming tick prices or claims of tick-level realtime service.
  • Trading signals, forecasts, recommendations, portfolio advice, or opportunity rankings.
  • Order entry, trade execution, money transfer, or brokerage functionality.
  • User portfolios, positions, account balances, or personal financial profiles.
  • Backtesting, historical strategy simulation, or predictive modelling.
  • A commercial availability guarantee or production trading SLA.
  • Unbounded ingestion of arbitrary ODP portfolios in the first release.

4. Contract-driven differentiator

The central differentiating requirement is that the application shall not merely display prices. It shall use a machine-readable data-product contract to explain what the product and companion contracts declare, what was observed, which deterministic rule was applied, and what conclusion follows.

Governed bundle declaration
→
Runtime observation
→
Deterministic evaluation
→
GPT-5.6 explanation

Declared

ODPS product identity and commitments plus responsibility-separated interface, instrument, rule, monitoring, publication, and application declarations.

Observed

Actual service responses, bar values, UTC timestamps, coverage, coherence, cache state, and evidence mode.

Evaluated and explained

Rule-by-rule deterministic results followed by a model explanation that cites the evidence and preserves unknowns.

Accepted product boundary Live data is the service; contract-driven trust and explanation are its augmented value.

5. ChatGPT-native experience

ODP Market Steward shall expose a few precise powers that ChatGPT can invoke at the right moment. It shall not reproduce an external website, navigation hierarchy, or trading terminal inside the conversation.

One-sentence job Interrogate live FX market data and the data-product contract that governs it without leaving ChatGPT.

Know

Bring current completed-bar values, runtime quality evidence, and governed bundle declarations into the conversation—context base ChatGPT does not possess.

Do

Retrieve, normalize, validate, calculate, and assess evidence. The first release intentionally performs no external write, trade, order, or account action.

Show

Render the market board, selected-bar details, state, and rule evidence more clearly than prose while keeping the conversation primary.

Selected powers

  1. Show the coherent current FX-35 completed-bar market.
  2. Explain one instrument using server-calculated descriptive metrics.
  3. Assess runtime evidence against the governed data-product contract.
  4. Answer bounded product-discovery questions with stable evidence references.
Design consequence The model owns conversational routing and explanation. The MCP server owns retrieval, normalization, calculations, rules, and structured evidence. The component is used only when it materially improves comprehension over a concise text answer.

6. Users and principal use cases

Analyst

Views the current completed-bar market, selects an instrument, and receives transparent movement and range calculations.

Governance specialist

Checks whether the snapshot satisfies the declared product contract and asks for exact rule and evidence references.

Developer

Discovers product metadata, access routes, expected fields, quality rules, limitations, and machine-readable results.

7.5 Runtime components

  • The existing FXLive Cloudflare Worker remains the independently deployed, read-only market-data service. Its public OpenAPI surface is consumed unchanged.
  • A separate ODP Market Steward Cloudflare Worker shall expose the public HTTPS Streamable HTTP /mcp endpoint, load and verify the governed bundle, call only the fixed FXLive origin, execute deterministic rules, and optionally call the OpenAI Responses API.
  • A per-MCP-session Cloudflare Durable Object shall coordinate short-lived acknowledgement state; stateless Worker memory shall not be treated as durable session state.
  • The ChatGPT host selects tools and narrates structured results. The Apps SDK component renders the disclosure and market evidence through the MCP Apps bridge and shall not call FXLive or OpenAI directly.
  • A private R2 bucket is conditional on written recording and redistribution rights. Without those rights and a verified immutable manifest, recorded fallback remains unavailable.

8. Functional requirements

“Shall” indicates required behavior. Acceptance criteria describe the minimum observable evidence needed before a requirement can move from Proposed to Verified.

8.1 Live market-data service

FR-LIVE-001 Present the market board first Must Proposed

After the MCP service verifies the required one-time app-session acknowledgement, the system shall return a market-data view as the primary result for a request to show the current FXLive market.

  • An unacknowledged gated call performs no FXLive or OpenAI request and returns DISCLOSURE_REQUIRED with the disclosure-capable component.
  • A verified acknowledgement is reusable only within the same MCP session and policy version.
  • The result contains the selected or standard-set market values before extended governance explanation.
  • The view states that values are latest completed one-minute bars rather than streaming ticks.
  • The view contains an observation timestamp and bar interval in UTC.
FR-LIVE-002 Retrieve the exact standard set Must Proposed

The system shall request and represent each member of the versioned FX-35 set exactly once for a full-board request.

  • Expected, returned, missing, duplicate, and unexpected identifiers are reported.
  • The application does not silently substitute or omit an instrument.
FR-LIVE-003 Establish a coherent completed-minute snapshot Must Proposed

The system shall avoid scoring a mixed-minute snapshot as one coherent market state.

  • Metadata is read before and after the instrument fan-out.
  • Both metadata observations and every accepted bar identify the same completed BarEnd.
  • One bounded reread is allowed during a rollover; continued mismatch returns INCOHERENT.
  • Retrieval availability, service state, freshness, fitness, plausibility, provenance, and bundle validation remain separately typed outcomes.
FR-LIVE-004 Refresh without misrepresenting recency Must Proposed

The system shall provide a user-visible manual refresh action and replace evidence only after the new result is complete.

  • The previous result remains visibly timestamped while refresh is in progress.
  • The component supplies its prior BarEnd; the server returns INITIAL, SAME_BAR, NEW_BAR, or NOT_EVALUATED when no comparable bar exists. Protocol and dependency failures use the separate typed error contract.
  • Manual refresh is the MVP default. Automatic refresh, if later enabled, is visible, pausable, and limited to an active component.
FR-LIVE-005 Expose partial and unavailable results honestly Must Proposed

The system shall never convert a partial, unparseable, unreachable, or no-evidence retrieval into a complete live market board.

  • Partial results identify missing instruments and failed checks.
  • Unreachable or unparseable service responses produce UNAVAILABLE.
  • Cached or recorded values are never relabelled as newly fetched live values.
  • A no-evidence result contains no market values and includes attemptedAtUtc plus a typed reason.

8.2 Instrument detail and descriptive calculations

FR-BAR-001 Display a selected completed bar Must Proposed

The system shall display the selected instrument, UTC interval, granularity, Open, High, Low, and Close.

  • The instrument belongs to the exact FX-35 enum.
  • The displayed numbers are the normalized server values used by the rule engine.
FR-BAR-002 Calculate declared OHLC metrics Must Proposed

The server shall calculate descriptive metrics using the formulas governed by rules/ohlc-rules.yaml and referenced from the ODPS executable-quality entries.

closeMinusOpen = Close − Open basisPointMovement = 10000 × (Close − Open) ÷ Open absoluteRange = High − Low rangeBasisPoints = 10000 × (High − Low) ÷ Open upperWick = High − max(Open, Close) lowerWick = min(Open, Close) − Low bodyToRangeRatio = abs(Close − Open) ÷ (High − Low), or 0 for zero range crossBarGap = Current Open − previous completed-bar Close
  • Open is the denominator for both basis-point calculations.
  • The displayed bar timestamp and instrument accompany every metric result.
FR-BAR-003 Use deterministic decimal arithmetic Must Proposed

The server shall calculate and round financial values using a declared decimal policy rather than binary floating-point display assumptions.

  • The rounding mode and displayed precision are defined before implementation is accepted.
  • Display precision respects the instrument value and does not force every instrument to the same number of decimal places.
  • The model receives completed values and does not recalculate them from prose.
FR-BAR-004 Require a genuine preceding bar for gap analysis Should Proposed

The system shall return NOT_EVALUATED for cross-bar gap when the immediately preceding completed bar is unavailable.

  • A non-consecutive or merely older value cannot be used as the previous close.
  • The prior and current bar intervals are returned as evidence when the metric is computed.

8.3 Deterministic data quality and fitness

FR-QUAL-001 Validate required fields Must Proposed

The system shall fail the completeness rule when any declared completed-bar field is absent or null.

  • The result identifies each missing or null field.
  • Dependent calculations are not produced when their required inputs are invalid.
FR-QUAL-002 Validate mathematical OHLC integrity Must Proposed

The system shall enforce the declared notation, granularity, UTC boundary, duration, positive-finite, and OHLC-ordering rules.

  • Currency matches six uppercase letters and belongs to FX-35.
  • Granularity equals 1m.
  • BarEnd − BarStart equals exactly 60 seconds.
  • Open, High, Low, and Close are finite and greater than zero.
  • Low ≤ Open, Low ≤ Close, High ≥ Open, and High ≥ Close.
FR-QUAL-003 Evaluate freshness only in context Must Proposed

The system shall apply the two-minute freshness rule only when an authoritative UTC market calendar reports OPEN.

  • When OPEN, both current time minus CreatedAt and current time minus latest BarEnd are no greater than two minutes.
  • An age of exactly 120 seconds passes; an age greater than 120 seconds fails while OPEN.
  • The origin, parsing, and evidence role of CreatedAt are defined and contract-tested before this rule is verified.
  • When CLOSED, timeliness is NOT_APPLICABLE.
  • Without an authoritative calendar result, timeliness is UNKNOWN.
  • Staleness alone is never used to infer that the market is closed.
FR-QUAL-004 Separate plausibility from validity Should Proposed

The system shall treat statistical unusualness as contextual review evidence, not as a hard-invalid bar or trading signal.

  • Plausibility states are NORMAL, UNUSUAL, and NOT_EVALUATED.
  • No result is produced until a UTC lookback window, minimum sample size, and alert threshold are configured.
  • An unusual result preserves the hard-validity result.
FR-QUAL-005 Assess fitness for a stated purpose Should Proposed

The system shall calculate a purpose-specific fitness result from deterministic evidence without producing an opaque numeric trust score.

  • Supported result states are FIT, FIT_WITH_CONDITIONS, NOT_FIT, and INDETERMINATE.
  • Every result identifies the stated purpose, decisive rules, evidence, and limitations.
  • Missing required evidence produces INDETERMINATE, not an assumed pass.
  • A successful /health response alone can never produce FIT or OPEN.
  • Every snapshot assessment states that it is point-in-time evidence rather than proof of longitudinal reliability or SLA performance.

8.4 Governed bundle and ODPS product governance

FR-ODP-001 Load and verify the governed bundle Must Proposed

The system shall load bundle.yaml first and verify every required versioned runtime artifact before presenting a governed-valid status.

  • Resolution follows the manifest load order, remains inside the bundle root, and verifies declared versions, dependencies, relative references, and SHA-256 digests.
  • Results identify product ID, product version, ODPS version, bundle version, and the relevant artifact digests.
  • A missing, invalid, or hash-mismatched required artifact prevents the app from presenting a governed-valid status.
  • Market values may remain viewable when governance is unavailable, but no governed FIT verdict may be issued.
FR-ODP-002 Resolve behavior by artifact responsibility Must Proposed

The application shall resolve each fact and behavior from its responsibility-owning artifact rather than treating ODPS as a universal execution, monitoring, AI, or presentation contract.

  • ODPS owns product declarations and commitments; OpenAPI owns HTTP behavior; the value set owns membership; rules own calculations and verdict logic; monitoring owns runtime state; publication policy owns disclosure; the MCP application contract owns tools and component behavior; and technical requirements own deployment architecture.
  • A companion artifact cannot silently override an ODPS product declaration or another companion’s assigned responsibility.
  • The same governed facts are not maintained independently in UI-only configuration.
  • A contract change produces an explicit version or compatibility change, not silent behavior drift.
FR-ODP-003 Answer governed product-discovery questions Should Proposed

The system shall answer bounded questions about identity, users, use cases, access, SLA, quality, instruments, analysis, session policy, and disclaimer.

  • Each returned declaration includes the owning artifact ID, path, stable pointer, version, and digest; ODPS pointers are used only for ODPS-owned facts.
  • Unknown or omitted product facts are reported as unavailable and are not invented.
FR-ODP-004 Separate validation layers Must Proposed

The system shall report syntax, controlling-schema, semantic-policy, cross-reference, source-alignment, and pinned ODP SDK compatibility as separate validation layers.

  • Each applicable artifact-layer result identifies artifact ID and path, layer, status, validator and version, evaluation UTC, evidence, and nullable schema URI/hash.
  • Supported statuses are PASS, FAIL, WARNING, NOT_APPLICABLE, and NOT_TESTED.
  • Every runtime artifact receives syntax, semantic-policy, and cross-reference results; any missing or failed required layer prevents a governed FIT result.
  • A compatibility warning is not silently converted into either an SDK pass or an ODPS failure.
  • The application never claims ODPI certification.
  • The exact validator and schema versions accompany results.

8.5 GPT-5.6 data steward

FR-AI-001 Generate an evidence-grounded Steward Brief Must Proposed

When includeStewardBrief=true, the MCP service shall make a bounded server-side OpenAI Responses API call using gpt-5.6 to convert validated structured evidence into a concise professional explanation after deterministic assessment completes.

  • The brief contains a governed answer, decisive evidence references, limitations, and a safe next step.
  • The model receives calculated values and rule results; it does not own the calculations or hard verdict.
  • The requested and actual response model identifiers are visible in implementation evidence and the typed result.
FR-AI-002 Preserve declaration, observation, and inference boundaries Must Proposed

Natural-language answers shall distinguish governed artifact declarations, runtime observations, deterministic evaluations, and model-authored explanation.

  • Recorded evidence is never described as current.
  • Missing evidence remains explicitly unknown.
  • The explanation cites rule IDs and observation timestamps supplied by the server.
FR-AI-003 Tailor explanations to professional intent Should Proposed

The steward shall explain the same evidence at an appropriate level for an analyst, governance specialist, or developer without changing the underlying result.

  • Role selection changes terminology and emphasis only.
  • If the requested purpose is materially ambiguous, the steward asks one bounded clarification rather than guessing.
FR-AI-004 Keep deterministic service available without the model Must Proposed

A GPT-5.6 timeout, malformed output, or unavailable model shall not prevent the market board or deterministic quality result from being returned.

  • The Steward Brief has typed states NOT_REQUESTED, UNAVAILABLE, REJECTED, and GENERATED.
  • A failed or rejected Steward Brief is labelled without altering, suppressing, or replacing data or deterministic verdict states.
  • Generated output is schema-validated and rejected if it changes deterministic facts or cites evidence that was not supplied.

8.6 Market state and recorded evidence

FR-TIME-001 Use UTC on every public temporal field Must Proposed

Every public timestamp shall use RFC 3339 UTC notation with a trailing Z.

  • Bar start, bar end, evidence retrieval time, recorded capture time, and calendar evaluation time are separately identified.
  • Host locale and host timezone do not alter parsing, calculation, comparison, or display.
  • No local timezone label appears on a public app surface.
FR-STATE-001 Classify service state in UTC Must Proposed

The server shall classify OPEN, MARKET_CLOSED, DEGRADED, UNAVAILABLE, or UNKNOWN through the complete ordered decision table in Section 10 using service evidence and an authoritative UTC calendar.

  • Holiday, daylight-saving, and maintenance behavior comes from the configured calendar rather than a hardcoded local opening hour.
  • The classification basis is returned with the state.
  • A LIVE badge is permitted only when the calendar is OPEN and the completed bar passes the declared freshness rule.
FR-STATE-002 Keep state dimensions separate Must Proposed

The system shall represent availability, service state, freshness, fitness, plausibility, provenance, and bundle validation as separate dimensions and shall not collapse them into one “trust” or “live” state.

  • When an authoritative calendar reports closed, the system may show evidence retrieved during the current call with its original bar timestamp, MARKET_CLOSED, and freshness NOT_APPLICABLE.
  • LIVE means current-call retrieval provenance only; it does not by itself imply open, fresh, complete, valid, or fit.
FR-STATE-003 Provide an immutable recorded fallback Must Proposed

When the request explicitly permits recorded fallback and written capture, storage, public-display, and redistribution rights have been approved, the system shall be able to display a captured, immutable 35-instrument evidence bundle when current live evidence cannot support the demonstration.

  • The manifest contains capture UTC, original bar intervals, product, bundle, policy, and artifact versions, product and content digests, response hashes, validation result, retention class, create-only write protection, and immutable manifest digest.
  • The component displays the canonical banner Recorded demo snapshot — captured {capturedAtUtc} — not current market data., where capturedAtUtc is RFC 3339 UTC ending in Z.
  • Recorded data cannot produce a current-freshness pass.
  • Without rights approval or a verified immutable manifest, the service returns no evidence rather than creating or using a recorded fallback.
FR-STATE-004 Support a clearly labelled synthetic anomaly replay Should Proposed

The automated test harness may replay a synthetic fixture containing a known missing, stale, incoherent, or mathematically invalid observation.

  • The fixture is visibly labelled SYNTHETIC TEST FIXTURE.
  • It is never mixed with or presented as live evidence.
  • It is never described as recorded market data.
  • It is not accepted as a public request mode and is not returned to ordinary plugin users.
  • The expected rule failure and actual result are covered by an automated test.

8.7 ChatGPT component and public presentation

FR-UI-001 Render one coherent market-data component Must Proposed

The ChatGPT component shall present evidence provenance, bar interval, retrieval or attempt time, availability, service state, freshness, fitness, market values when present, quality summary, and disclaimer as one coherent result without collapsing those dimensions into a single badge.

  • The primary values are readable without opening a nested dashboard.
  • Detailed rule evidence is progressively disclosed rather than hidden.
  • No nested page, trading terminal, or duplicate chat composer is embedded.
FR-UI-002 Provide bounded component actions Must Proposed

The primary inline result shall expose no more than two prominent actions: refresh evidence and explain the selected result.

  • Market, refresh, and explanation actions invoke read-only domain tools; only the explicit disclosure control may invoke the bounded component-only acknowledgement action.
  • Sorting or instrument selection does not generate an investment recommendation.
FR-UI-003 Make state understandable without color Must Proposed

Every state and verdict shall be communicated using text and, where appropriate, an icon in addition to color.

  • Keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic labels, and WCAG AA contrast are tested.
  • The market board remains usable on ChatGPT web and mobile layouts.
FR-PUB-001 Display the complete demo disclaimer Must Proposed

Every public tool result and component shall include the MARKET DATA DEMO label and complete approved disclaimer.

  • The full statement is model-visible and user-visible, not only hidden in component metadata.
  • The disclaimer appears in public specification, catalog, app UI, screenshots, video, and README.
  • Interactive market-data use requires a one-time acknowledgement before the first retrieval in accordance with the publication policy and MCP application contract.
FR-PUB-002 Enforce public identity policy Must Proposed

Public artifacts shall identify ODP Market Steward, FXLive, and QMVP only and shall not disclose upstream source identity.

  • A configured prohibited-term scan covers server responses, component bundle, screenshots, README, and video script.
  • This requirement is a release gate only when the applicable data rights permit omission of attribution.
FR-PUB-003 Prevent advice and execution behavior Must Proposed

The system shall not generate trading signals, forecasts, recommendations, orders, or execution instructions.

  • Movement comparison remains descriptive and is not framed as an opportunity ranking.
  • Negative prompt tests verify refusals or safe reframing for prohibited financial actions.

8.8 ChatGPT-native discovery and orchestration

FR-CHAT-001 Deliver value in the first meaningful response Must Proposed

For a user with no prior brand awareness, the first meaningful app response shall explain its role in one line, deliver useful output immediately, and offer one clear next step.

  • The response does not begin with a feature tour, navigation menu, or multi-step onboarding form.
  • A valid general market request returns concrete market evidence on the first tool-assisted turn.
  • The role statement identifies completed-bar market data and governed evidence without claiming advice, certification, or official ODPI endorsement.
FR-CHAT-002 Handle vague and specific intent conversationally Must Proposed

The app shall use relevant thread context, act directly on complete requests, and ask no more than two essential clarifying questions for materially ambiguous requests.

  • A specific request does not ask the user to repeat an instrument, purpose, metric, or evidence mode already present in the conversation.
  • A vague request starts useful progress whenever a safe default such as the standard market board is available.
  • Optional refinements follow the initial result rather than blocking it.
  • The app never infers a trading objective from a general market-data question.
FR-CHAT-003 Expose clear and accurately annotated tools Must Proposed

Every MCP tool shall have a unique verb-led name, a plain-language description of when it applies, standalone JSON Schema input and output contracts, and annotations that match its observed behavior.

  • Advertised schemas contain no references into an enclosing YAML document, and each handler’s accepted input and returned structuredContent match the advertised schema.
  • Descriptions state what the tool does and do not manipulate selection, disparage alternatives, or recommend triggering beyond explicit user intent.
  • The five model-visible domain tools declare readOnlyHint: true and destructiveHint: false; the component-only acknowledgement tool declares readOnlyHint: false, destructiveHint: false, and non-idempotent single-use challenge behavior.
  • External FXLive retrieval is represented with the applicable openWorldHint and a submission justification consistent with actual network behavior.
  • Direct, indirect, and negative prompt tests demonstrate correct tool selection and non-selection.
FR-CHAT-004 Minimize model-to-tool data transfer Must Proposed

Tool inputs shall request only structured fields required for the selected market-data or governance job.

  • No tool accepts full conversation history, raw transcripts, arbitrary context blobs, credentials, precise location, or unrelated personal data.
  • Instrument, purpose, metric, evidence mode, and requested governed-product section use bounded enums or narrowly validated values.
  • A brief task-specific intent string is accepted only where it materially improves a governed explanation and is covered by the privacy policy.
FR-CHAT-005 Return concise and composable structured results Must Proposed

Each tool shall pair a brief model-readable content summary with stable structuredContent that another conversational step or app can reuse and component-only _meta only where necessary.

  • Structured results use stable field names, instrument IDs, RFC 3339 UTC intervals, rule IDs, evidence mode, and ODPS pointers where relevant.
  • Important facts are not available only as prose or only inside component-only metadata.
  • Tool-visible and component-hydration responses omit secrets, credentials, signing keys, raw telemetry, and diagnostic traces; component-only challenge material is short-lived, signed, and not authorization by itself.
  • Schema evolution is backward compatible within a published plugin version.
FR-CHAT-006 Return control to the conversation Should Proposed

Each capability shall complete one bounded job and hand control back to ChatGPT rather than forcing a tunnel-like proprietary workflow.

  • The user can complete at least one meaningful end-to-end market-data task without leaving ChatGPT.
  • Outputs remain usable when another compatible app or later ODP Market Steward capability is called next.
  • The component does not reproduce ChatGPT navigation, history, confirmation, or composer functions.
FR-EVAL-001 Measure incremental app value Must Proposed

The release evaluation shall compare app-assisted answers with base ChatGPT answers for the same versioned prompt set.

  • The set covers direct, vague or indirect, negative, and edge-case prompts for live data, product discovery, quality evidence, and prohibited financial behavior.
  • A documented rubric scores factual grounding, current-data access, correct tool routing, structured completeness, state honesty, first-turn usefulness, and policy compliance.
  • The report records per-case results and an app-versus-base win rate without hiding ties or failures.

8.10 Consent, evidence requests, and Cloudflare delivery

FR-ACK-001 Gate market-data retrieval on acknowledgement Must Proposed

The MCP service shall not call the FXLive market-data service until the user has acknowledged the exact current publication-policy disclaimer in the active MCP session.

  • Initial tool output may provide policy text, a signed acknowledgement challenge, and the component, but contains no retrieved market values.
  • The component invokes a component-only acknowledgement tool to mutate session policy state, then reissues the previously validated pending market request after an ACKNOWLEDGED result for user continuity.
  • The challenge binds the active session, exact policy version, exact disclaimer digest, nonce, and expiry. The pending request is not authorization; the server validates the post-acknowledgement call normally and does not rely on the component preserving it unchanged.
  • The service rejects missing, expired, replayed, modified, or cross-session acknowledgements with a typed non-retrieval result.
  • Acknowledgement records a safety disclosure; it is not identity verification, authorization, suitability assessment, or financial consent.
FR-ACK-002 Maintain bounded session acknowledgement state Must Proposed

The service shall maintain acknowledgement and replay-protection state in a strongly consistent per-session runtime object with automatic expiry.

  • Stored fields are limited to policy version, disclaimer digest, challenge nonce or hash, issue and expiry times, acknowledgement time, and replay state.
  • No prompt text, market-data response, user profile, or ChatGPT transcript is stored in acknowledgement state.
  • An expired session requires a new challenge and acknowledgement before another upstream retrieval.
  • Signing secrets remain server-side and never appear in tool content, component metadata, logs, or public artifacts.
FR-EVID-001 Use an explicit evidence request and result matrix Must Proposed

Every market-data operation shall separate the requested evidence policy from the evidence actually returned.

  • Public request policy is LIVE_ONLY or ALLOW_RECORDED_FALLBACK; synthetic fixtures are test-only and cannot be requested through the public tool surface.
  • Public result mode is LIVE, RECORDED, or NONE; LAST_AVAILABLE is reserved and prohibited until a separately governed meaning is approved.
  • NONE includes a typed reason such as acknowledgement required, upstream unavailable, invalid response, rights not approved, or verified recording unavailable.
  • A recorded result can be selected only when the caller permits it and the policy, rights, integrity, and availability gates all pass.
FR-DEP-001 Operate a dedicated Cloudflare MCP service Must Proposed

The app backend shall run as a separate Cloudflare Worker that exposes a stable public HTTPS Streamable HTTP /mcp endpoint and a non-sensitive health endpoint.

  • The new service uses the fixed public FXLive origin through its OpenAPI contract and does not read the source service's KV namespace directly.
  • The existing FXLive Worker remains the market-data service and is not changed merely to expose an OpenAPI description.
  • The MCP service allows only declared upstream hosts, routes, methods, parameters, and instrument values.
  • Development, staging, and production configurations use separate names, secrets, state, and deployment evidence.
FR-DEP-002 Serve the versioned Apps SDK component through MCP Must Proposed

The MCP service shall register the disclosure-capable market component as a versioned MCP UI resource and return it from every gated market-data tool.

  • The component resource uses a versioned ui:// URI and the required MCP App HTML media type.
  • Its declared content-security policy permits only the minimum required origins; the component does not fetch FXLive or OpenAI directly.
  • Model-visible structured content is sufficient for a correct text answer even when component rendering is unavailable.
  • A production publication candidate declares a unique component domain and passes ChatGPT developer-mode rendering tests.
FR-DEP-003 Use strong session coordination without retaining user content Must Proposed

A Cloudflare Durable Object shall coordinate each active MCP session when session-bound acknowledgement or replay protection is required.

  • One session maps deterministically to one session object and receives serialized state transitions.
  • Alarm- or TTL-based cleanup removes expired acknowledgement state.
  • Cloudflare KV is not used for replay-sensitive acknowledgement state because this requirement needs strong per-session consistency.
  • The service remains read-only with respect to financial systems and performs no trade, order, account, or portfolio action.
FR-DEP-004 Keep recorded-evidence storage conditional Should Proposed

Immutable recorded market evidence may be stored in Cloudflare R2 only after written rights and an approved retention and publication policy exist.

  • The first implementation shall not persist live no-store responses in caches, KV, Durable Objects, logs, or analytics.
  • When enabled, each recorded object has a manifest with capture UTC, content hash, product, bundle, policy, and artifact versions, validation result, retention class, create-only write protection, and an immutable manifest digest.
  • Recorded objects use create-only conditional writes; overwrite and mutation of an existing evidence key are prohibited.
  • Missing authorization or integrity evidence disables recorded fallback and returns result mode NONE.
  • D1, Queues, Vectorize, and Workers AI are not required for the MVP unless a separately approved requirement justifies them.

10. State and verdict model

The application shall avoid one opaque “trust score.” Artifact validity, service state, fitness, and plausibility are separate dimensions.

Dimension Allowed states Purpose
Artifact validation layer PASS, FAIL, WARNING, NOT_APPLICABLE, NOT_TESTED Reports every applicable layer independently; controlling-schema validity and SDK compatibility are never collapsed.
Availability AVAILABLE, PARTIAL, UNAVAILABLE, INCOHERENT Describes retrieval completeness and snapshot coherence.
Service state OPEN, MARKET_CLOSED, DEGRADED, UNAVAILABLE, UNKNOWN Combines calendar and mandatory runtime evidence according to the declared policy.
Fitness verdict FIT, FIT_WITH_CONDITIONS, NOT_FIT, INDETERMINATE Answers whether the observed product is usable for the stated purpose.
Plausibility NORMAL, UNUSUAL, NOT_EVALUATED Provides non-fatal contextual review evidence.
Evidence mode LIVE, RECORDED, NONE States what evidence was actually returned. LAST_AVAILABLE is reserved and prohibited; synthetic fixtures are test-only.

Deterministic request and evidence workflow

  1. Validate the request and current session acknowledgement. Failure returns evidence mode NONE and performs no upstream retrieval.
  2. Verify the governed bundle and retain its status as a separate dimension; an invalid bundle prevents a governed fitness verdict but does not rewrite observed market values.
  3. Attempt current retrieval. On failure, select RECORDED only when the request permits it and the rights, policy, integrity, and availability gates pass; otherwise return NONE.
  4. Normalize the selected evidence and derive availability: missing or unparseable evidence is UNAVAILABLE; incomplete coverage is PARTIAL; mixed bar intervals are INCOHERENT.

Total service-state classification order

  1. An unreachable or unparseable API result produces UNAVAILABLE.
  2. A parseable response with no usable market evidence produces UNAVAILABLE.
  3. An unavailable authoritative UTC calendar produces UNKNOWN.
  4. An authoritative closed calendar produces MARKET_CLOSED and freshness NOT_APPLICABLE.
  5. An open calendar plus a mandatory rule FAIL produces DEGRADED.
  6. An open calendar plus any mandatory UNKNOWN or NOT_EVALUATED result produces UNKNOWN.
  7. An open calendar plus all mandatory rules passing produces OPEN.
  8. Any other parseable state produces the safe default UNKNOWN.

Purpose-specific fitness is evaluated after service state and remains an independent dimension.
